Bottoms-up reality check

What a "sliver" is actually worth.

Assume a conservative ~0.6% net take rate (we charge under 1%, and keep roughly half to two-thirds after liquidity and payout costs). Here's what different share levels produce — starting tiny.

The point isn't the exact figure — it's the leverage. A few percent of one corridor is a venture-scale business; the rail and compliance you build to win it are then reusable across every other corridor at near-zero marginal cost. That's the platform.

The rail is no longer theoretical

We're not betting on stablecoins. We're riding a wave that already broke.

The single biggest risk to a thesis like this used to be "will the infrastructure exist?" In 2025 that question got answered. The rail is large, regulated, and growing fast.

And critically — regulatory clarity arrived with it. The US GENIUS Act, the EU's MiCA, and the UAE's VARA/ADGM frameworks mean this can be built as a licensed, compliant business, not a grey-market experiment. Timing is the whole game, and the window just opened.
The bigger picture

Remittance is the wedge — not the ceiling.

The same rail, wallet and licensed network that move a worker's wages home can, over time, carry merchant settlement, SME cross-border payments, payroll, and savings products. Remittance is simply the highest-frequency, most emotionally urgent way in. Land it, and the surface area for everything after is enormous.
