The whole company in one sentence

Setu is Wise-grade pricing on a stablecoin rail, aimed squarely at the Gulf → South Asia corridor, with the crypto hidden so completely that the customer just sees money arrive.

Why we exist

A 6% tax on people who can least afford it.

Migrant workers send a slice of every paycheck home. The middlemen — banks, correspondent networks, Western Union — quietly skim 5–7% and take days. We remove almost all of it.

01

The pain is universal & recurring

A worker sending AED 1,000 monthly loses ~$17 every time — roughly $200 a year, forever. It's felt, repeated, and hated.

02

The rail finally exists

Regulated stablecoins now settle value across borders in seconds for fractions of a cent. The expensive correspondent-bank chain is simply… deleted.

03

The trick is hiding it

Every prior crypto-remittance attempt asked grandma to understand wallets. Setu's entire UX is "send money." The chain is an invisible engine.

Why now — three tailwinds converging

This was impossible two years ago. It's inevitable now.

§Regulation

Clear stablecoin law

The US GENIUS Act and the EU's MiCA gave stablecoins a real legal home. The UAE's VARA & ADGM offer explicit licensing. You can now build this inside the rules.

↗Adoption

Emerging markets lead

APAC is the fastest-growing region for on-chain activity, with India, Vietnam & Pakistan driving it. The users are already here — they just need a product that hides the complexity.

⚡Infrastructure

UX caught up

Account abstraction, passkeys and embedded wallets killed seed phrases. A person can now use a stablecoin without ever seeing one — the missing piece for mass adoption.
