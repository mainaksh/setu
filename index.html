The problem, in plain words

Sending money home is slow, and the fee is brutal.

A construction worker in Dubai sends AED 1,000 (about $270) to his family every month. Between the transfer fee and a bad exchange rate, roughly $17 quietly disappears. That's a day's food for the family — gone, every single month, to middlemen.

Under the hood · the technical bits

Two layers, so a person never has to "get" crypto.

Layer 1 — Settlement (value-stable)

Value crosses borders as a regulated stablecoin (USDC), so a sender's money is never exposed to crypto volatility. We use an established, regulated stablecoin rather than minting our own — instant trust, far less regulatory burden, faster launch.

USDC stablecoin Stellar / Solana / Base L2 sub-cent network fees

Layer 2 — Network token (SETU)

SETU is the plumbing for the people who run the rails: liquidity providers stake it for the right to fill payouts and earn fees, it grants fee rebates, and it governs corridor settings & treasury. A grandmother sending money never has to see it, hold it, or understand it.

The money math · how <1% is even possible

Where the fee goes on a $270 transfer.

The reason banks charge 5–7% is layers of correspondent banks and a hidden FX markup. Setu strips that to almost nothing — the only real cost is a thin FX spread and the local cash-out.

The SETU token

A utility token for the network — not a get-rich pitch.

SETU exists to coordinate and reward the people who supply liquidity and run the rails. Its value comes from real fee capture and staking demand, not marketing. Common users never need to buy it.

  • 35% — Liquidity & agent rewards. Emitted over years to bootstrap the LP flywheel.
  • 25% — Ecosystem & growth. Corridor launches, merchant onboarding, referrals.
  • 20% — Team & advisors. Long vesting (4yr, 1yr cliff) to align incentives.
  • 12% — Treasury / reserve. Governed on-chain for stability & runway.
  • 8% — Public / community. Fair, capped distribution.
Said plainly: SETU is never marketed to senders as an investment. That separation — stable money for users, utility token for operators — is both the compliance-safe design and the reason adoption isn't gated on speculation.
Where it lives · you said location is free

Home base: Abu Dhabi (ADGM), United Arab Emirates.

For this corridor, the UAE is the single wisest pick — and the choice is strategic, not just tax-driven.

🛬 It sits on the corridor

Millions of South-Asian workers live and earn in the Gulf. Your sender base is physically next door to your HQ — ideal for cash-in, support, and trust.

⚖️ Clear crypto law

ADGM and Dubai's VARA offer mature, explicit licensing for virtual assets and stablecoin activity — you build inside the rules, not in a grey zone.

🏦 Banking + no income tax

Strong stablecoin-friendly banking rails and a business-friendly tax regime keep operating costs and friction low.

The honest legal reality — read this before spending a rupee. India taxes crypto gains at 30% plus a 1% TDS on transactions and regulates payment/remittance flows tightly (RBI / FEMA). That is exactly why Setu issues and operates from the UAE and reaches India only through licensed payout partners — never as an unlicensed money transmitter inside India. None of this page is legal or financial advice. Before launch you must engage qualified counsel in every jurisdiction you touch, obtain the right VASP / payment licenses, and build a real AML program. Skipping this is how projects get shut down, not scaled.
How this gets made live

From idea to a working corridor, in five phases.

PHASE 0 · weeks 0–12

Legal & foundations

Set up the ADGM entity, engage crypto counsel, design the AML/KYC program, and sign a stablecoin + banking partner and an India payout partner. Nothing ships before this is real.

PHASE 1 · months 3–6

MVP · one corridor (UAE → India)

A clean mobile app: cash-in in the UAE, USDC rail, payout into Indian bank/UPI via partner. Liquidity handled manually at first. Goal: the first 1,000 real senders who feel the savings.

PHASE 2 · months 6–12

Liquidity network + SETU utility

Open up the LP/agent program with SETU staking so liquidity scales without us holding all the float. Spreads tighten as more LPs compete.

PHASE 3 · months 12–18

Merchant acceptance loop

QR payments at local shops in receiving regions, closing the earn-and-spend loop and deepening on-the-ground demand.

PHASE 4 · month 18+

New corridors

Replicate the proven playbook: UAE→Pakistan, UAE→Philippines, UAE→Bangladesh, then Saudi and Qatar origins. Compliance re-checked for every new lane.
