Technology

Boring for the user.
Serious engineering underneath.

The product feels like a simple money app precisely because the architecture does the hard work. Four layers — app, network token, settlement, and ramps — combine into a system that's instant for the user and compliant for the regulator.

System architecture

Four layers, one job: local money in, local money out.

LAYER 4

App & identity

The mobile app the customer touches: onboarding, KYC, saved recipients, rate quotes, receipts. All values shown in local currency. This is the only layer the user ever perceives.

The genius is the separation: the user-facing layers speak money and never mention crypto, while the settlement layer speaks stablecoin and never touches a customer. Each does one thing well.
Chain selection

We're chain-agnostic — but we choose deliberately.

The settlement chain must be fast, near-free, final, and carry deep regulated-stablecoin liquidity. We abstract the chain away from the app, so we can switch or multi-home without the user noticing. Here's the candidate comparison.

ChainTypical feeFinalityUSDC supportWhy it matters here
Solana<$0.01~secondsnativeHigh throughput & low cost; strong for consumer payments at scale
Stellar~$0.00001~5snativePurpose-built for cross-border payments & remittance

Decision: launch on a low-fee, fast settlement chain with native, regulated USDC and deep liquidity (Solana or Stellar as lead candidates), behind a chain-abstraction layer so the rail can evolve without touching the product. We optimise for cost-per-transfer, finality, and stablecoin depth — not ideology.

Fee/finality figures are approximate and chain-dependent; final selection pending technical due diligence and partner liquidity terms.

Why we don't mint our own coin

We use a regulated stablecoin. Trust is borrowed, not built from scratch.

🏛️

Already regulated

USDC operates under emerging frameworks (US GENIUS Act, EU MiCA) with attested, transparent reserves. We inherit that credibility instead of spending years earning it.

⚙️

Less to defend

Minting our own stablecoin means managing reserves, redemptions, audits and reserve-banking risk. That's a whole company. We'd rather perfect the rail and the experience.

💧

Instant liquidity

An established stablecoin already has deep markets and partners everywhere, so liquidity providers can source and offload it cheaply from day one.

The customer never sees "USDC" anyway — so there's no branding upside to our own coin, only cost and risk. The SETU token is the network's coordination layer, not a stablecoin (full detail on the Token tab).
Wallets & custody

No seed phrases. No lost funds. No crypto homework.

The single biggest barrier to crypto adoption was key management. Account abstraction and passkeys remove it entirely — the user gets bank-like recoverability with self-custody-grade security.

🔑 Account abstraction

Each user gets a smart-account wallet that can be controlled by a passkey/biometric rather than a raw private key — programmable, recoverable, and human-friendly.

🧬 Social / passkey recovery

Lost your phone? Recover access via passkey + verification, the way you'd reset a normal account — no 12 words to lose forever.

🛡️ Funds segregation

Customer balances are held distinctly from operating funds; stablecoin held in transit is never commingled with company treasury.

⛽ Gas abstraction

Network fees are sponsored and paid by Setu in the background. The user never holds a "gas token" or sees a fee they don't understand.

The liquidity engine

The hard part isn't the transfer. It's the cash on both ends.

Moving USDC is trivial. The real engineering is matching every transfer to local-currency liquidity at the tightest spread, instantly. That's what the routing engine + LP network do.

🔁

Competitive spreads

LPs bid to fill payouts. The engine routes to the cheapest, so competition — not us — drives the price down.

⚖️

Float management

The engine balances liquidity across corridors, pre-positioning where demand is, minimising idle capital.

🎯

SETU-staked access

LPs stake SETU for the right to serve high-value flow, aligning their incentives with network reliability.

Security model

Money apps live and die on trust.

🔍 Audited contracts

All smart-contract components independently audited before mainnet, with continuous monitoring and a bug-bounty program.

📡 Real-time monitoring

On-chain transaction monitoring and anomaly detection flag suspicious flows automatically, 24/7.

🔐 Key management

Operational keys in hardware security modules / MPC with multi-party approval for treasury movements.

🧯 Limits & circuit breakers

Per-user and system-level velocity limits and kill-switches contain any incident before it spreads.

Compliance, built in

Regulators are a feature, not an afterthought.

A money-movement business that isn't compliant isn't a business — it's a liability. The compliance stack is wired through the whole system from day one.

🪪

KYC / identity

Document + biometric verification at onboarding via a specialist provider; risk-based re-verification thereafter.

🧭

AML & screening

Sanctions, PEP and watchlist screening on every party; transaction-pattern analysis to detect structuring or fraud.

🔗

Travel Rule

Originator/beneficiary data shared between regulated parties as required for cross-border value transfer.

⛓️

On-chain analytics

Blockchain-forensics tooling screens stablecoin addresses against illicit-activity databases before settlement.

📁

Audit trail

Every transfer fully logged and reconstructable for regulators and auditors across both fiat and on-chain legs.

🏛️

Licensed perimeter

Operates within VASP / payment licensing in the UAE and via licensed partners in payout markets (see Roadmap & Invest).
