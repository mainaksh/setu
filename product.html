Product

Send money.
The crypto stays invisible.

The entire product can be described in two words to a customer: send money. No wallets, no seed phrases, no "buy a token." Underneath, a regulated stablecoin does the heavy lifting — but the person never has to know, and never has to care.

The design doctrine

Every prior crypto-remittance app failed the same way: it made grandma learn crypto.

Setu's product philosophy is a single rule — the user's mental model is a money app, full stop. Four principles enforce it.

1

No keys, ever

Embedded smart wallets and passkeys mean there's no seed phrase to lose. You log in like any app; the keys are managed and recoverable behind the scenes.

2

Local currency, always

You see AED going in and ₹ coming out. The USDC in the middle is never shown, never named, never your problem. Numbers are in money you understand.

3

One screen, one action

The home screen has one job: send. Amount, recipient, confirm. Everything advanced is buried where only power users go looking.

4

Honest, upfront rate

The exact amount the family receives — and the exact fee — is shown before you confirm. No hidden FX markup. Trust is the product.

The second loop · merchants

Once money lands, let people spend it.

A worker who receives — or holds — value in Setu can pay local shops by QR. The merchant settles instantly and cashes out same-day, for far less than card networks charge. This closes a local economic loop and deepens demand.

Reaching the unbanked · agents

Not everyone has a bank. The M-Pesa lesson.

A network of local cash-in / cash-out agents — shops, kiosks, exchange houses — lets people deposit and withdraw physical cash. This is how mobile money reached hundreds of millions in Africa, and it's how Setu reaches workers without bank accounts.

💵

Cash in

Hand cash to a local agent; it appears in your Setu balance instantly. No bank account required to start sending.

🏪

Cash out

The receiving family can collect physical cash from a nearby agent — vital where digital payout isn't an option.

🤝

Agents earn

Agents earn a commission on every cash-in/out and become local champions of the product — distribution that markets itself.

What's in the app

Simple on the surface. Complete underneath.

🔐

Invisible KYC

Identity verified once at onboarding — required for compliance, designed to feel like a normal sign-up.

👨‍👩‍👧

Saved recipients

Family saved like contacts. Repeat sends are two taps.

🔒

Rate lock

The quoted rate is held while you confirm — no nasty surprise on settlement.

🌍

Multi-language

Hindi, Malayalam, Urdu, Tagalog, Arabic, English — meet users in their language.

📊

Limits & safety

Sensible per-tier limits and fraud monitoring, mostly invisible to honest users.

🎁

Referrals

Invite family and friends; both sides get a fee credit. Growth wired into the product.
