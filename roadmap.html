The phases in detail

What actually happens, and when.

Phase 0 · Legal & foundations

WEEKS 0–12
Goals
  • Incorporate the ADGM entity in Abu Dhabi
  • Engage crypto/payments counsel in UAE + India
  • Design the AML/KYC compliance program
  • Sign stablecoin, banking & India payout partners (LOIs)
What gets de-risked
  • Confirms a legal, licensable path exists
  • Locks the jurisdiction & corporate structure
  • Secures the partners the whole model depends on
Gate to Phase 1: counsel sign-off on the operating model, entity live, and at least one binding payout-partner agreement in place.

Phase 1 · MVP corridor (UAE → India)

MONTHS 3–6
Goals
  • Ship the sender app (iOS + Android)
  • Cash-in in the UAE; USDC rail; payout to India via partner
  • Onboard the first 1,000 real senders
  • Liquidity handled manually / by a single partner at first
What gets de-risked
  • Proves real people send real money repeatedly
  • Validates the <1% price and instant settlement live
  • Generates first cohort retention & CAC data
Gate to Phase 2: demonstrable repeat usage (a real retention curve), settlement reliably under target time & cost, and clean compliance operations.

Phase 2 · Liquidity network + SETU utility

MONTHS 6–12
Goals
  • Open the LP/agent program with SETU staking
  • Routing engine auto-matches transfers to cheapest LP
  • Cash-in/out agent network in key UAE locations
  • Scale toward tens of thousands of active senders
What gets de-risked
  • Liquidity scales without Setu fronting all the float
  • Spreads tighten as LPs compete — better price & margin
  • Proves the network can grow beyond a single operator
Gate to Phase 3: majority of payouts filled by third-party LPs, spreads trending down, agent network functioning.

Phase 3 · Merchant acceptance loop

MONTHS 12–18
Goals
  • QR payments at local shops in receiving regions
  • Merchant app + same-day cash-out
  • Close the earn → spend → cash-out loop
  • Approach contribution break-even (~150k active senders)
What gets de-risked
  • Adds a second high-volume revenue stream
  • Deepens on-the-ground demand & stickiness
  • Proves the full unit-economics picture at density
Gate to Phase 4: positive contribution margin demonstrated; merchant loop showing measurable retention lift.

Phase 4 · Multi-corridor platform

MONTH 18+
Goals
  • Open UAE→Pakistan, then Bangladesh & Philippines
  • Add Saudi/Qatar as new origin markets
  • Reuse rail, wallet & compliance; swap only ramp partners
  • Raise Series A on proven corridor economics
What gets de-risked
  • Proves the playbook replicates at a fraction of the cost
  • Demonstrates platform leverage & compounding growth
  • Establishes the path to the full SAM
Beyond: the same rail extends to payroll, SME cross-border, and savings — remittance was always the wedge.

What could go wrong — and what we do about it

The honest risk register.

Every serious investor asks this; showing it unprompted builds trust. The biggest risks aren't technical — they're regulatory and liquidity.

RiskSeverityMitigation
Regulatory change / licensing delayHIGHDomicile in a clear-regime jurisdiction (ADGM), counsel from day one, reach receiving markets only via licensed partners.
Liquidity / payout reliabilityHIGHStart with a committed partner; build the LP network & routing engine to diversify; SETU incentives to attract liquidity.
Incumbent price responseMEDStructural cost advantage on a cheaper rail; incumbents are anchored to correspondent/cash economics they can't easily abandon.
Stablecoin / chain riskMEDUse a regulated, transparent stablecoin; chain-abstraction to switch rails; never expose customers to volatility.
Adoption slower than hopedMEDBeachhead a single dense corridor; lean burn; referral + agent distribution to keep CAC low while iterating.
